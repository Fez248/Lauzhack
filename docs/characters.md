This is Tea [DCST] --> Name of our app

In order to make our application funny and easy to use we plan to have
some characters be the ones that teach you and guide you through your
jounrney. Let me present them to you:

## Cat
This is the first one you meet, the cat is a she. She is not selfish, but
she believes that she can not help other if she doesnt help herself first.
That is why she is the one that will be teaching you about your personal
digital safety, how to be secure first on an individual basis. She is
sharp, intelligent, relies on facts but knows that emotions are a big
thing and that is why she knows how to control them. So no one can
manipulate her. She knows herself very well. She will be teaching all sorts
of personal cybersecurity practices like:
    - Password manager
    - Save passwords
    - Don't repeat passwords
    - Phishing emails
    - Which information does it make sense for a site to ask you about and
      which ones does not
    - Which sites to visit, what to download
    - Basically put you in a zero trust mindset

## Turtle
On the other side we have the turtle. The turtle is also a she. She is very
focused on the common good and cares on how to secure groups. She thinks
that a good base security for the entire team is what makes a company
secure. Kind off like vaccinating does not only help you but also helps
your community. We need an environment that we can trust, it builds family.
She is very intelligent aswell, cares a lot about people and their well
being. She is also very all, that is why she has so much experienec. She 
would be the one teaching you how to secure your organization, which are
good practices, which are bad ones. Example:
    - When someone is going to be laid off their access and accounts should
      suspended before notifying him he is going to be suspended. As a lot
      of cybersecurity incidents involve an unhappy worker inside the 
      company.
    - Etc, more things related to company cybersecurity that know I can't 
      think of.
    - having systems up to date
    - having action plans and knowing what would would need to be done in
      case of an accident.
    - simulations, etc

## Dog
The dog, he has the dog in it xd. The dog is a he. He has a lot of friends,
a lot of connections, a lot of experience, and has been into many many
situations like this before. That is why he is very wise, very intelligent,
and well connected, always knows what to say. Thus, he is the one giving
you tools, mainly free and open source, that help you secure yourself and
your organization. Example:
    - VirusTotal
    - HaveIBeenPwned?
    - IsITPhishing

## Spider
Lastly, we have the spider. The spider is a he. He is very intelligent too,
he is very elusive, machiavellian, he knows how to move, how to surf the
web (xdd, beacuse he is a spider yeah). He is the one in charge of trying
to fish you. The one that will try to catch you and will. Once it does it
will show you how it did it and what resulted in you falling for it.
Examples of attacks:
    - Mail phishing with a slightly different domain from us asking for 
      password
    - Or a website asking for data it should not be asking, like we tell
      you we dont charge but we ask you for your credit card, or for your
      location or any other data that has nothing to do.
    - Or we try to impersonaet someone by extracting information
    - Or we send you a malicious sms or file
    - We play with your feelings
