## What do we teach?

We target cybersecurity from two different perspectives. We educate people
on their personal security, how to put good passwords, how to manage them,
what to trust, what should they not trust, we teach them to be digitally
responsible. That is the foundation of what we want to achieve. It doesn't
matter if you have an amazing architecture and the latest auth tools if
your employeers write their passwords on sticky notes next to their laptops
:).

The second thing we teach, that is built from that, is how to secure your
organization. We help them identify bad practies, and we help them
implement good ones. For example, when an employee is laid off, his
accounts should be deactivated, and access removed. Even more, this should
be done before telling him he is being laid off. This is just an example.

We also provide a list of free tools that can help someone take care of
their online security.

## How do we teach it? What is our differenciator?

Well, we have light theory, teached on a light fast-paced way. Kind of
duolingo, some trivial questions. 

Then we have a way of teaching more difficult and extensive concepts [to be
thought on how to do this].

And then, we have our interactive teaching, it is so interactive, that the
student doesn't even know he is taking part on it. We try to hack him, not
directly, but using social engineering. Let's say we send him a phishing
email, we try to impersonate someone, etc. After we do it it then appears
in the next lesson, with the resutls that we got. 

Example: For the first lesson, even before that lesson is taken, we send
a phsihing email from a domain close to ours but not the same, saying that
there has been a problem with the account creation or whatever. Then, if
they fall and give us their credentials on a fake domain that appears on
the first lesson. Hey, we sent you this, you fell for it. That way, next
time our student will be more alert, but we would change our method, nexxt
we send an smsgs, or we impersonate someone, or we wait a month unitl the
nexxt one. the goal is to induce the student into an always alert state,
which is the one they should be anyways. Our for example, they put sensitive
information about their company into an LLM.

That is how we do it.
